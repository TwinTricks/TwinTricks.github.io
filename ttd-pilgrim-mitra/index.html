TTD Pilgrim Mitra

A free Chrome extension that keeps your family's pilgrim details on your own device, encrypted behind a PIN, and fills the official TTD booking form when you ask it to.

What it does

  • Saves name, age, gender, photo ID proof and number for each pilgrim.
  • Fills the pilgrim details step of a TTD darshan, seva or accommodation booking.
  • Groups the people who travel together, so a whole family fills in one press.

What it never does

  • Send pilgrim details anywhere. There is no account, no server and no analytics.
  • Enter an OTP, solve a captcha, choose a slot, or pay. Those stay yours.
  • Book a ticket for you, or improve your chances of getting one.
